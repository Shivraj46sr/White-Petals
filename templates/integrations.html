Back up or restore CRM data

Create a consistent SQLite snapshot while the CRM is running. A daily backup is also scheduled at {{ backup_time }} Asia/Kolkata while the app is running. Backups stay in the local, git-ignored backups folder and a manual snapshot downloads to your browser. Keep a separate copy in a secure location. A database backup contains prospect and customer records; it does not include .env secrets. The scheduled job pauses if the computer sleeps or the app stops.

Email & WhatsApp

Drafts can be reviewed and copied, but sending, OAuth, inbound replies, and provider status tracking are not implemented. Do not add Gmail or WhatsApp passwords here.

Use official Gmail OAuth or the official WhatsApp Business Platform if you commission those integrations. Messaging must be authorized and respect opt-outs.

AI drafts & agent tasks

Gemini runs only when you explicitly request a draft, analysis, or agent task. The AI workspace sends limited lead business fields, not contacts, CRM notes, website contents, or communication history. Your agent instructions and task text are also sent, so do not include secrets or personal information. Google may process requests under your account and applicable terms; review privacy and cost before use.

Store GEMINI_API_KEY in the local, git-ignored .env file and restart the app. Agent outputs are saved for human review; agents cannot change CRM records, browse, or send messages.

Deployment: this app binds to 127.0.0.1 by default. Before exposing it to a network or cloud, configure a strong persistent SECRET_KEY and APP_PASSWORD, use HTTPS, and add production-grade authorization and deployment controls.
