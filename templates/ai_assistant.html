Agents can analyze selected CRM business fields and prepare plans, research checklists, priorities, follow-up recommendations, or outreach drafts. They cannot browse the web, change CRM records, contact customers, send messages, or confirm bookings. Their task results are saved here for your review.

Gemini receives the agent name, specialty, guidance, task, and up to 25 active lead summaries (company/category/industry/location/stage, score, follow-up, estimated value, research status). Contact details, notes, website contents, and communication history are excluded. Do not put secrets or personal data in custom instructions or tasks. Google may process requests under its API terms and charges. Verify facts and consent before acting.
